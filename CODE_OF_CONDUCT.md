# Code of conduct

We want this project to be a welcoming, professional place for everyone, whatever their background, identity or experience.

We follow the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). In short:

- **Do:** be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, accept feedback gracefully, and focus on what's best for the people using the action.
- **Don't:** harass, insult or demean anyone, make sexual or discriminatory remarks, publish someone's private information, or behave in a way that would be inappropriate at work.

This applies in this repository's issues and pull requests, and anywhere you represent the project.

## Reporting

If you see or experience behavior that breaks this code, email `dpo@sbombox.com`. Reports are handled confidentially by the SBOMBox team, and we'll respect the privacy of whoever reports.

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues or block people who break this code, following the Contributor Covenant's [enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
